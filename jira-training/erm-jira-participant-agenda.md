# Jira Training — Session Agenda
## Enterprise Risk Management Team | 60 Minutes

---

**What this session is:** A hands-on introduction to using Jira Cloud for the ERM project. By the end, you will be able to create and link work items, navigate the project, understand status conventions, and run basic searches.

**What this session is not:** An agile training. We do not use sprints, story points, or velocity. This session is focused entirely on how the ERM team uses Jira to track and manage work.

**Come prepared with:** Jira Cloud access confirmed, and one real or realistic piece of work in mind that you could turn into a Story during the hands-on block.

## Views Reference

Jira Cloud offers four primary views of your work:

| View | Best used for |
|---|---|
| Board | Seeing active work by status across the team |
| Backlog | Planning and prioritizing unstarted work |
| List | Sorting, bulk-reviewing fields, finding tickets quickly |
| Plans | Viewing the full Epic → Feature → Story hierarchy on a timeline |

**Note on Plans:** Plans writes changes directly to live tickets when you drag items on the timeline. During the session, navigate and explore Plans but do not drag or reschedule items unless directed.

| Level | Timeframe | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Epic | 6 – 12 months | Major strategic initiative |
| Feature | 1 – 3 months | Significant deliverable within an Epic |
| Story | 1 – 3 weeks | Discrete, assignable unit of work |

All ticket IDs follow the format **ERM-001**, **ERM-002**, etc.
